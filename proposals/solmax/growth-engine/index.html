The ask

One meeting. One chance to set the agenda.

“What can you support me with? How can I show up looking and feeling prepared?” Karen Lubbe · 26 August 2026

Bob has called the senior team to Longueuil on September 16th, 8–12, to set 2027 objectives, key results and owners. He has told the room exactly what to bring: specific actions, sized opportunities, solid data — and growth. “You can't shrink your way to greatness.”

What you hold

The strongest hand in the room

A vertical build programme with Mining already proving the model, a CRM lead engine going live region by region, thirty engineering tools, and 1,500 website leads in H1. The raw material is all there.

The problem

It lives in a dozen files

The story is scattered across decks, spreadsheets and email threads — and the CRM story has a weak flank: strong adoption in AMS, poor in EMEA and APAC. Unassembled, it reads as activity. Assembled, it reads as a growth plan.

The job

One argument, with numbers

Turn what you already have into a single storyline in the language Bob uses — OKRs, sized opportunities, lead generation, opportunity qualification, order realization — with every figure sourced.

Six days of work, starting Monday 31 August. The materials are already in hand, and MissionCTRL carries the load — your role is advisory: a kick-off call, a review call and a rehearsal, around three hours in total. Solmax provides the data; MissionCTRL turns it into the argument. Everything lands before you fly to Montreal.
