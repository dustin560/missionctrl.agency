    The starting position

    Solmax doesn't have an awareness problem. It has a self-awareness problem.

    You said it clearly in our conversation: Solmax is operating in the dark.

    Decisions on gut feel. Regions running their own P&Ls. Five or more legacy businesses stitched together. A brand architecture live only since the end of 2024.

    All the right bets are being made. But there is a gap between the strategy and the stakeholder — and until you can measure it, you cannot close it.

    That gap has a name: integrity. The alignment between what Solmax commits to, what stakeholders believe it stands for, and what they actually experience.

    The good news: Solmax has not lost trust so much as it has not yet built it deliberately. Very different starting position. Real opportunity.

    What 58 means
    Functional

    Trust is real, but uneven. Some relationships carry the business; others are quietly costing it — and the difference only shows under change.

    • Your claims are accepted — but they are checked first, and that check has a cost in time and margin.
    • Strong relationships are subsidising weak ones. Because it averages out, nobody sees which is which.
    • Change is expensive, because belief has to be rebuilt each time rather than carried over.

    Above 70, trust starts absorbing shocks for you — you get the benefit of the doubt, and claims land first time. That is the move worth making.

    What we see

    Connection is the thinnest layer — the gap between the rebuild and the story people hear. And the rebuild has not slowed: three more regional leaders landed this summer, in EMEA, Asia Pacific and the Americas. Six senior appointments in twenty-one months.

    The challenge

    Every move is defensible. But each one reaches stakeholders without a story attached — and it shows. Employer sentiment sits at 3.1 out of 5. The faster the transformation moves, the wider the gap opens.

    Why it matters

    Solmax is not an outlier. Across twelve sectors we have read, Connection is the weakest layer in eleven — trailing Clarity by about 18 points. Solmax is running slightly ahead of that pattern. The opportunity is to break it entirely.

    How to read the 58. It is an estimate inferred from your public footprint — news, policy pages, trade press, investor disclosures, employer reviews. No stakeholder has been surveyed to produce it. Measuring properly is what replaces it with a number you can take to a board.

    The ambition

    Solmax as a movement, not just a manufacturer.

    When we asked how Solmax should define itself — geosynthetics manufacturer, or protector of land and water — Karen's answer pointed at something bigger than a positioning statement.

    Ørsted did not just change its own business. It decarbonised a category and rebuilt communities. That is what operationalising a purpose looks like — not campaigns, but every interaction, consistently, over time.

    Solmax has the same potential: the world's largest geosynthetics manufacturer, working where sustainable construction, land protection and water security meet. Get the promise right and you don't just win deals. You shape how the category thinks.

    The difference between a company and a movement is integrity at scale: when what you believe, what you say, and what you do are the same thing — and stakeholders can feel it.

    Step 02

    Baseline & the wave cycle

    Once live, TrustOS reads every four months: updated scores by stakeholder group, a "what changed" narrative tied to real events, the driver breakdown, and the actions that follow.

    Not a one-off audit. An operating rhythm — leadership gets a reliable signal, year on year. And the co-design continues: the instrument evolves with you each quarter, with every change assessed for its effect on wave comparability before it lands — so the trend line stays honest.

    How we listen — surveys are the smallest part of it

    The instrument reads trust wherever the relationship already speaks. Surveys fill the gaps — they don't carry the load. Every source is weighed for what it adds to Clarity, Connection or Confidence before it earns a place in the read. And because trust is a trend, the guiding philosophy is sustainable listening: methods chosen for how well they repeat, wave after wave, riding inside workflows Solmax already runs — adapted as slightly as possible — rather than asking anyone to do new work.

    • Public signal. Reviews, employer ratings, media sentiment, tender and award outcomes — twelve sectors already read this way, nobody asked anything.
    • Data you already hold. NPS, CSAT, engagement, retention, churn — measured once by someone else, reused by TrustOS. No duplication.
    • Behavioural signal. Repeat-order rates, quote-to-order conversion, tool usage, vertical-page engagement. An engineer who runs your calculators twice a month is telling you something no survey can — and your new CRM lead flow makes it readable.
    • Embedded micro-pulses. One question in the flow — after a quote, a delivery, a training session, a trade-show scan — on the channels each group actually uses, WhatsApp included for distributors and installers.
    • AI-moderated conversations. Short adaptive interviews, chat-based, run in any language at scale — richer than a survey, a fraction of the fieldwork cost.
    • The moments of truth. Win/loss debriefs, exit conversations, and frontline sentiment logged by your own teams — the places people say what they actually think.

    TrustOS ingests any signal you can export. Which sources carry Solmax's read — and in what mix per stakeholder group — is decided together at the data assessment in Step 01, and evolves wave by wave.
